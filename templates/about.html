Our Methodology

SpoofGuard AI utilizes a sophisticated hybrid approach to face authentication. By combining temporal analysis of video streams with high-frequency spatial analysis of static images, we ensure that the person behind the camera is genuine.

MobileNetV3 Backbone

Our image analysis module leverages the MobileNetV3 architecture, optimized for high accuracy on edge devices without compromising on speed.

ResNet Temporal Fusion

For video and live streams, we use a temporal fusion network based on ResNet to detect micro-jitters and liveness indicators that are impossible for deepfakes to replicate.
